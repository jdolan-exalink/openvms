# Live Stream Quality Switching

## Objective

Provide a short-lived high-detail stream switch from the live camera sidebar and automatically show the main stream after a camera remains maximized, without introducing a visible blank frame or losing the low-resolution stream.

## Problem and rationale

Live supports separate `_sub` and `_main` streams. A quality change currently selects one stream/session, so switching can incur startup delay or a blank interval. Operators need a quick 30-second detail view and a main-stream view while a camera is maximized.

## Scope and constraints

- Add a camera-row control beside the name that expands quality controls for a camera currently in Live; allow switching every active grid view of that camera from `_sub` to `_main` for 30 seconds, then restore each view's original quality.
- After a camera remains maximized for more than 5 seconds, show `_main`; when unmaximized, restore `_sub`.
- Keep both streams alive during transitions and retain the currently visible stream until the target stream has produced its first frame, avoiding a black/blank cut.
- Do not preload a second stream solely as a hidden speculative buffer; the user explicitly requests both streams remain alive during the transition/maximized interval.
- Preserve persisted grid/view quality preferences; temporary quality overrides must not overwrite saved layout state.
- Key timers and temporary state to camera/tile identity and cancel them safely on close, replacement, maximize changes, and unmount.
- Strict TDD is enabled by project instructions; test runner: `cd apps/web && pnpm test`.

## Acceptance criteria and checks

- Clicking the camera-row control exposes the running camera's quality choice; selecting main leaves every active grid view of that camera on main for 30 seconds, then restores each view's original quality.
- Maximizing for 5 seconds or less does not trigger the automatic quality change; remaining maximized beyond 5 seconds activates main and unmaximize restores sub.
- The sub stream remains live while main starts, and the visible surface changes only after main's first decoded frame; no blank intermediary is introduced.
- Handoff readiness is defined at the rendered target-surface boundary: MSE readiness must include a decoded/drawable frame and non-opaque target status chrome must not cover the base before handoff; fisheye readiness must wait until WebGL has painted its first output frame, not merely video loadeddata/videoWidth.
- Temporary changes do not alter saved grid/view layout quality.
- Closing/replacing the camera or unmounting cancels timers and releases both sessions without stale callbacks resurrecting either stream.
- Focused tests, full `pnpm test`, `pnpm typecheck`, changed-file lint, and `git diff --check` pass.
